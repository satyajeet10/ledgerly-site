Frequently asked questions

Can I import expenses from another tool?

Yes. Upload a CSV from any expense tool and map the columns once; Ledgerly remembers the mapping.

Where is my data stored?

In data centres in India, encrypted at rest and in transit.

Can I change plans later?

Any time. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades take effect at the next billing date.
